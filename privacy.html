Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the 963 Aqar mobile app and website (“963 Aqar”, “we”, “us”) collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), preferred language and optional profile photo.
  • Social sign-in: if you sign in with Google or Apple we receive your name and email address from that provider (with Apple you may choose to hide your email).
  • Listings: property details, photos, price, contact numbers and the location you pin on the map. Photos are compressed and their EXIF metadata (including GPS data) is removed on your device before upload.
  • Messages and reports: what you send through Contact Us, account deletion requests, and reports about listings.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage of listings: views, calls, WhatsApp taps, shares and favourites on listings, used to show owners aggregate statistics.
  • Favourites, saved searches and notifications you create in the app.
  • Device token: a push-notification token for your device, removed when you sign out.
  • Technical data: like any online service, our servers and providers process your IP address and basic device and request information to deliver the service and keep it secure.

We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell your personal information.

2. Location

The app uses your device location only when you tap “My location” on the map or when you pin a property’s location while creating a listing. We do not track or store your personal location in the background. The location of a property you publish is stored with the listing and shown publicly on the map.

3. How we use information

  • To create and run your account and sign you in.
  • To publish your listings, review them before they go live, and remove listings that break our Terms.
  • To let people contact advertisers, and to show owners statistics about their listings.
  • To send notifications you asked for (listing approval, saved-search matches, listing reminders, replies).
  • To answer your messages and requests.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and abuse, and to keep the service secure.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it.

4. What other users see

When you publish a listing, its details, photos, location, your display name, profile photo and the contact number you add to the listing are public. The phone number on your account and your email address are never shown to other users.

5. Service providers we share data with

We share information only with providers who help us run 963 Aqar, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage (hosts account data, listings and photos).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service — delivering push notifications.
  • Google and Apple — only if you choose to sign in with them.
  • Map tile providers (OpenStreetMap, Esri, OpenTopoMap) — your device requests map images from them, which reveals your IP address and the map area being viewed.

When you tap Call or WhatsApp on a listing, your phone app or WhatsApp opens and their own privacy terms apply. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data on servers located outside your country. We rely on providers that apply industry-standard safeguards to protect it.

7. Retention

  • Account data is kept while your account is active.
  • Listings stay until you delete them. Sold or rented listings stay visible with a badge for a limited time, then are hidden from the public but kept in your account until you delete them.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, listings, photos, favourites, saved searches, notifications and device tokens are permanently deleted. Messages you sent to us may be kept, without a link to your account, as long as needed to handle them.
  • Backups maintained by our providers are overwritten on their regular schedule.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correct: view and edit your profile and listings in the app at any time.
  • Delete: delete individual listings at any time, or delete your entire account from Profile → Settings → Delete account in the app, or through our account deletion page.
  • Notifications: turn push notifications off in your device settings.
  • Location: deny or revoke location permission in your device settings; the app still works.
  • For any other request about your data, contact us.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to it is restricted by row-level security rules so users can only change their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

963 Aqar is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date above, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Contact Us page or from Profile → Contact us in the app.
